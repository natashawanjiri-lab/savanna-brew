Our Story

Savanna Brew Cafe started in 2021 with a simple dream: to bring the best Kenyan coffee to the heart of Westlands. What began as a small kiosk with two tables is now a warm, welcoming cafe where friends meet, students study, and neighbours catch up.

We work directly with smallholder farmers in Nyeri and Kiambu. Every bean is hand-picked, sun-dried, and roasted in small batches in Nairobi so you always get a fresh, rich cup.

Our Mission

To serve honest, delicious Kenyan coffee in a space where everyone feels at home.
